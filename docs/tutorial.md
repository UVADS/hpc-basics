# Take the Tutorial

UVA Research Computing (RC) offers an **Intro to HPC** orientation for new users: live (synchronous) sessions and self-paced materials on the [RC Learning Portal](https://learning.rc.virginia.edu/). 

Schedules, registration, and recordings are listed on RC's [Training](https://rc.virginia.edu/training) page. 

**New users are strongly encouraged to attend a live Intro to HPC session or watch the recording** before diving into Open OnDemand and batch jobs on this site.
