# Contacting RC support

Open a ticket through Research Computing’s support channels:

- [Support overview](https://www.rc.virginia.edu/support/) — status, office hours, and entry points
- [Support request form](https://www.rc.virginia.edu/form/support-request/) — general HPC, storage, Ivy, and related questions

When you contact support, include:

- Your **computing ID**
- Cluster or service (e.g. Rivanna/Afton, storage, Open OnDemand)
- **Job ID** (if relevant) and partition/account
- What you tried and the **exact error message** or screenshot
- Paths to input/output files when useful (not passwords or secrets)

RC also holds **virtual office hours** (see the [support page](https://www.rc.virginia.edu/support/)) for project questions and consultations.
